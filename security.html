How to bank safely

Protect your login

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, and never share login details.

Avoid phishing

Verify all messages and emails before clicking links. MDS Bank will never request your password by email.

Secure your OTP

Your one-time codes are confidential. Do not share them with anyone, even if the request appears urgent.

Monitor account alerts

Enable notifications for every transaction so you can spot unusual activity immediately.

Report suspicious activity

Contact support right away if you see unauthorized transactions, strange messages, or login issues.
