Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my online banking password? +

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You'll need your username and email to receive a reset link.

What are the routing numbers for MDS Bank? +

Our routing number for domestic transfers is 123456789. For international wires, use SWIFT code MDSBXXXX.

How do I dispute a transaction? +

Log in to your dashboard, click on the transaction, and select "Dispute this charge".
