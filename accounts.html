Account FAQs

What do I need to open an account?+

You will need a valid government-issued ID, your Social Security Number, and proof of address. The application takes less than 5 minutes online.

Are there any monthly maintenance fees?+

Our Everyday Checking and High-Yield Savings accounts have zero monthly fees. Premium Checking requires a $5,000 minimum combined balance to waive the $15 monthly fee.

How does early direct deposit work?+

When your employer submits payroll files, we make funds available immediately — often up to 2 days faster than traditional banks.
