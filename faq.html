Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open an MDS Bank account? +

You can open an account online by visiting our Apply page and completing the simple application form. Prepare a valid ID and proof of address.

How do I reset my online banking password? +

On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and follow the prompts. You will receive a reset link via the email address on file.

What should I do if I lose my card? +

Please contact our support team immediately and request card suspension. You can also control card status through the mobile app once it is activated.

How do I apply for a personal loan? +

Visit the Loans page to review available loan products and use our online application process for a quick pre-qualification decision.

How can I keep my banking secure? +

Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords. Use strong device security and contact MDS Bank immediately if you suspect fraud.

Do you offer branch support for business banking? +

Yes. Our business banking team is available at select branches and via the corporate services page for payments, payroll, and merchant solutions.
