Frequently Asked Questions

Account

How do I open an MDS Bank account? +

You can open an account online by visiting our application page and completing the simple multi-step form. Prepare a valid ID and proof of address.

Can I open an account entirely online? +

Yes. Our online application allows you to choose a product, upload required documents, and submit your details without visiting a branch.

Cards

What rewards are available on credit cards? +

MDS Bank cards offer benefits such as cashback, travel rewards, and flexible spending categories depending on the card you choose.

Loans

How do I apply for a personal loan? +

Visit our loan application page to review options, use the EMI calculator, and complete the online application for quick pre-qualification.

Security

How can I keep my banking secure? +

Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords. Use strong device security and contact MDS Bank immediately if you suspect fraud or unauthorized access.

Digital Banking

Can I manage my accounts from mobile? +

Yes. You can access account details, transfers, payments, and card controls through the MDS Bank mobile app and online portal.
